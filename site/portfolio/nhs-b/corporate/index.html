Why Partner with NHS?

The NHS Corporate Advantage

🏆

60 Years of Trust

NHS has been a trusted community institution since 1966. Associating your brand with ours connects you to six decades of goodwill in Northumberland County.

🌍

Community Visibility

Partners are recognized across our digital presence, social media channels, events, and new facility — reaching thousands of Northumberland residents.

💼

Tax-Deductible Giving

Corporate contributions to NHS qualify for charitable tax receipts. Our team handles all documentation for your finance team.
