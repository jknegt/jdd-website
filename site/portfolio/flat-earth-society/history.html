The True History (Probably)

Every pizza shop has an origin story. Most of them are lies designed to sell you a slice. This one, we're fairly confident, is not.

History, part 1

They don't want you to know why the pizza is round. We do.

Everyone knows the pizza is round because a chef somewhere spun some dough one day. That's the story they want you comfortable with. We looked closer. We found a different answer, and it involves a flat disc, a very committed founding committee, and a lot of late-night arguing about crust geometry.

History, part 2

Every founding story has a version they'll tell you, and a version that's actually true. This is the true one. Probably.

The official version says a small group of friends pooled some money, rented a storefront, and started selling slices. That part's true, technically. What the official version leaves out is why. Ask any of them and you'll get a different answer -- a dream, an argument, a dare, a map someone found and couldn't stop thinking about. We've stopped trying to reconcile the stories. We've started serving the pizza instead.

History, part 3

We didn't set out to open a pizza shop. The pizza shop found us. Wake up.

We didn't set out to run a restaurant. We set out to ask questions nobody else was asking, and somewhere along the way, a pizza shop happened to us. If that sounds backwards, you haven't been paying attention. Most good things happen backwards. Most true things do too.
