Building Compassionate Communities

Education & Outreach

We bring animal welfare education to schools, community groups, and families across Northumberland County.

Our Education Programs

🎓

School Programs

Interactive presentations for Kindergarten through Grade 8 covering animal care, responsible pet ownership, and bite prevention.

🏠

Shelter Visits

Bring your class or group for a guided shelter tour, meeting animals and learning about their care needs.

👥

Community Presentations

NHS staff available for presentations at community events, libraries, senior centres, and public gatherings.

📚

Resource Library

Free take-home materials on responsible pet ownership, humane education, and community animal welfare.
